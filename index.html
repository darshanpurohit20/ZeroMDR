05 / FAQ

Questions, answered.

What is ZeroMDR?

ZeroMDR is the working name for an educational SaaS concept focused on payment orchestration, fee transparency, routing and reconciliation.

Does ZeroMDR bypass UPI MDR?

No. The prototype should not split or disguise a payment to evade applicable MDR, transaction limits, reporting requirements or provider rules. A production implementation would need to follow the applicable NPCI, bank, PSP and legal requirements.

Why is the ₹2,000 threshold important?

Under the announced framework effective October 15, 2026, a 0.4% MDR applies to specified person-to-merchant UPI transactions above ₹2,000, subject to exemptions and sector-specific rules. Customers are not intended to be directly charged the MDR.

Can a merchant use ZeroMDR for payments?

The landing page is a product prototype. A real payment service would require appropriate partnerships, compliance, merchant onboarding, risk controls and authorization before processing live payments.

What payment methods can the concept support?

The UI is designed to accommodate UPI QR, UPI intent/deep-link flows, payment links, provider routing, transaction status, reconciliation and analytics. Actual availability depends on integrations and provider approvals.

Who is it for?

Small and large merchants, SaaS platforms, marketplaces and developers who need a transparent layer for payment collection and reconciliation.

Is this a real payment gateway?

No. This single-file page is a front-end product concept. It does not move money or connect to a live bank, UPI PSP or payment gateway.
